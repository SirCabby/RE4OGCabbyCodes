[size=6][b]RE4OGCabbyCodes[/b][/size]

A [b]cheat panel[/b] for [b]Resident Evil 4[/b] - the 2005 original, as the Ultimate HD Edition on Steam (app 254700). Pause the game and tick what you want - God mode, Ashley takes no damage, One hit kills, Infinite ammo, Steady aim, Automatic QTEs, Automatic shooting gallery, Endless combo, Kill enemies as they appear and Bonus time always on (the Mercenaries), Infinite money, Save without counting, Misses don't count, Deaths don't count, Freeze play time, Freeze countdown timer, editors for the save count, the play time, the countdown, your pesetas, the results screen's counts, the costume set and the difficulty, an [b]attache case editor[/b] (add any of the game's items, change counts, loaded rounds and upgrade levels) and a limitless [b]item box[/b] of the mod's own, kept outside the game. Beside the game's Load list and a typewriter's save list it is a [b]save file manager[/b]: delete a save, or copy one into another file.

This mod is open source! Check it out at [url=https://github.com/SirCabby/RE4OGCabbyCodes]https://github.com/SirCabby/RE4OGCabbyCodes[/url]

[size=5][b]What it does[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]God mode[/b] - no hit takes any health off you, and your health is held at its maximum. The hits made to kill you outright (an attack meant to kill in one blow, a falling rock, the truck) knock you down instead. You still get knocked about, and a grab or a scene that ends in a death of its own (a fall, a missed QTE) is left to the game.
[*][b]Ashley takes no damage[/b] - the same for Ashley, in the main game. What would kill her outright does not: an explosion close by (the catapults' shots in the castle, a grenade, dynamite) or the truck makes her duck, and El Gigante's stomp and a falling rock leave her be, as they leave her armour. Being carried off is not damage - the game ends that its own way.
[*][b]One hit kills[/b] - any hit that does damage takes all of an enemy's health, so the game's own code kills it - deaths, drops and parasites as usual. El Gigante falls to the first hit anywhere, as it does to a rocket (a flash grenade still only blinds it). Krauser, whom the game keeps alive through the first part of his fight, flinches and retreats at the first hit with damage there; mutated, he falls to the first hit, even on the arm he guards with.
[*][b]Infinite ammo[/b] - the gun in your hands keeps its loaded rounds, and the bowgun and Krauser's bow use no bolts or arrows. Grenades and the rocket launcher are used up as usual.
[*][b]Steady aim[/b] - your aim holds still and your shots go where it points: the laser sight's dot and the view through a rifle's scope no longer drift about, a shot no longer throws the aim, and bullets fly straight down it with no random spread (handguns, magnums, machine guns, the mounted gun). Leon holds his aiming stance still, so the gun points where the laser does; once you stop moving the aim, the laser and your bullets keep that exact line through the recoil too, until you move it again. A shotgun keeps its spread of pellets, centred on the aim; a thrown grenade or egg gets no random sideways push.
[*][b]Automatic QTEs[/b] - the quick time events play themselves. A prompt to press buttons in time (dodging Krauser's knife or a boss's blow, the boulder, the statue's swings, the lasers, Saddler's bridges) succeeds the moment it comes up; where a prompt wants presses as fast as you can (running from the boulder, swimming back to the boat, climbing up, El Gigante's parasite, the end of Krauser's knife fight) the game sees a press every frame; running from the statue, Leon goes at full speed from his first step; a grab is shaken off at once. Cranks are no QTE and are left to you.
[*][b]Automatic shooting gallery[/b] - the merchant's shooting gallery plays itself for the most points it gives: every Ganado target in the head, never an Ashley target, every bonus target five hits in a row bring on, and, where the gallery offers game C or D, the two props worth 300 more. It keeps its hits in step with the streak so that no bonus target is lost. The targets fall as if shot; no shots are fired, so leave your gun be while it plays (a miss of yours ends the streak). The most each game gives, as the mod reads the game's code: [b]6300[/b] where the gallery offers game C or D (6400 for game D before you hold three of its caps), [b]6000[/b] where it offers only A or B.
[*][b]The Mercenaries[/b] - three switches of their own, shown only while you play the Mercenaries, with the combo, the kills and the score beside them. [b]Endless combo[/b]: a combo never runs out of time - every kill counts as the next one in it however long since the last, so its bonus climbs to its most (from the tenth kill in a row on) and stays there; what the combo earns goes onto your score as it is earned, each bonus shown as it comes, instead of waiting for a combo's end that never comes. [b]Kill enemies as they appear[/b]: every enemy dies the moment it is in play, wherever it is, as if shot - the game's own death, and the kill scores as yours do (its points, the combo, the kill count), the chainsaws, the Garradors and J.J. included. The game sends a stage's enemies only from the parts of it you stand in; with this on, every part sends its own wherever you are. They come at the game's pace: at most ten about at once. Every enemy and every body takes some of the game's model memory, and a new one the game cannot fit in it vanishes before it appears - lost to the stage for good - so the bodies go quickly (half a second of the fall, then they sink and fade at once), the oldest enemies die first, the kills wait in the rare moment the bodies about would leave no room for the enemies that come next (the panel says "waiting for bodies to clear"), and an enemy lost anyway is sent again once there is room. With every part sending, a house's enemies come from the start, so entering one brings nothing new; the village's last 11 come with three Bonus Time hourglasses in the game (once each is picked up) - with this on they come with the rest whether you pick them up or not. Its chainsaws come in pairs at 10, 25 and 35 kills, each pair only once, and one the game has no room for as its pair comes would never come; with this on it comes later with the rest. So all 124 come. The castle sends its 348 its own way: with every part sending at once it would send more in a moment than it has room for, and an enemy it has no room for is lost to it - so there its parts send in turn, a couple at a time, any that still find no room are sent again, and its bodies are cleared away the moment they have gone instead of every ten seconds. Two gates there open only when you go to them (one you walk up to, one you open with the action button) and let out 12 more, and the second also brings 25 later; with this on, once the castle has nothing left to send, each gate opens by itself as if you had gone there - the game's own short scene of the gate plays. So all 348 come. Waterworld sends from seven zones while you stand in them: each sends 15 (one 10) and is done, but one that has a single send left when you leave and come back sends five at once - 19 (14), the most it sends, and how the fastest runs reach 156. Its last 17 come once each from spots you have to reach (the tower's top among them), and one comes at 30 kills. With this on, the stage's spots are the mod's: each zone sends up to one short of its end and then its last five together, the spots send once each, wherever you are. So all 156 come - never more, as each zone still stops where the game stops it. It waits for the clock to start (with Bonus time always on, for that time to be running too) and stops when time is up or you are down; the mod's log notes the kills and the game's memory every 15 seconds, and what the stage has about if the kills stop for 12 seconds. [b]Bonus time always on[/b]: the Bonus Points time (the one a Bonus Points hourglass starts, in which every kill's combo bonus is at least 1,000) runs from the moment the clock starts and never runs out; what a combo earns goes onto your score once the combo is over instead of waiting for that time to end - or as it is earned, with Endless combo. Switched off, 30 seconds of it are left, as a pickup would leave.
[*][b]Save count[/b] - save without counting (saving adds nothing to the save count the load and save lists show for each file), or set the count, up to 999.
[*][b]Play time[/b] - freeze it, or set it (up to 99:59:59); the game counts on from what you set.
[*][b]Countdown timer[/b] - freeze or set the countdown on screen: the Mercenaries' clock and every timed scene of the game.
[*][b]Infinite money[/b] - the merchant takes no pesetas for what you buy or tune up; selling still pays. He still only sells what your pesetas cover.
[*][b]Pesetas[/b] - set your money, up to 99,999,999.
[*][b]Costume[/b] - switch the main game's costume set (Normal, Special 1, Special 2) mid-game; Leon changes at the next door.
[*][b]Difficulty[/b] - switch between Amateur, Easy, Normal and Professional on the spot, in the main game and Separate Ways.
[*][b]Results[/b] - deaths, enemies killed, shots fired and hits, for this chapter and the whole game, each editable, with the hit ratio. [b]Misses don't count[/b] (only a shot that hits counts as a shot fired) and [b]Deaths don't count[/b] (a death adds nothing to the chapter's or the game's deaths).
[*][b]Attache case editor[/b] - change a count, a weapon's loaded rounds or its upgrade levels (up to the exclusive upgrade), store an item in the game's item box (Assignment Ada and the Mercenaries have none), or throw it away. The case's size (S, M, L, XL) is shown with it - the case's own, not an item.
[*][b]Add to the case[/b] - inside the attache case's section, folding on its own: what takes room in the attache case (weapons, ammo, grenades, recovery items, attachments, and Assignment Ada's Plaga Samples), filed under tabs (weapons, grenades, attachments, ammo, recovery) and headings (handguns, magnums, shotguns, ...), with a search box that narrows the list as you type, by name or item number. The game's own placement finds a new item room in the case, turning it if it has to, as a pickup does. Key items, files and treasures are added from their own lists.
[*][b]Key items[/b], [b]Files[/b] and [b]Treasures[/b] - three lists of their own for what lives off the case's grid: the key items and maps you carry, the files (like the Playing Manuals), and the treasures, gems and bottle caps. Remove one, change a treasure's count, or add what you lack from each list's own Add (Add key items, Add files, Add treasures), inside its section and folding on its own, like Add to the case in the attache case's. They take no room in the case, so they never go in the item box. The shooting gallery counts the bottle caps you hold only while you hold its Bottle Caps collection too.
[*][b]Item box[/b] - the item box RE4 never had, with no limit, for what takes room in the case: Store an item out of your case, Take it back wherever the game finds it room (topping up what you carry first). Everything of one item is one entry however much you store (Handgun Ammo x350, handed back one piece's worth at a time); a weapon keeps its own entry, with its upgrade levels and loaded rounds, and nothing else has either. A stock or scope stored while it is on a gun comes back loose. Filed under the case's tabs (weapons, grenades, attachments, ammo, recovery) in the order Add to the case lists them, with a filter box; change an entry's count and Apply to set it to any number. [b]One box for each game that saves[/b]: the main game has its box and Separate Ways one of its own; every save file and new game of that game sees the same box, and nothing the game does changes it - not a save, a load, a new game or a continue. Each lives in the mod's own file (RE4OGCabbyCodes.itembox.ini, RE4OGCabbyCodes.itembox.separate-ways.ini), written at every change, so your save never holds any of it. Assignment Ada and the Mercenaries, played in one sitting with no saves, have no item box: the panel shows neither the box nor Store there.
[*][b]Save file manager[/b] - beside the title's Load list or a typewriter's save list: all twenty files with chapter, play time, difficulty and date. [b]Delete[/b] empties a file; [b]Copy to...[/b] copies a save into any other file, empty or not. Both ask first and write savegame00.sav at once, as the game does - a backup of the file is kept beside the mod first.
[*][b]A fix for the game: Separate Ways' clear save[/b] - always on. When you beat Separate Ways the game asks whether to save, and sometimes no file list comes up: "Saving..." shows for a few seconds, then the title, and no clear file is made. The cause is in the game: as the results come up it saves its records (the clear times) on their own, and when that save cannot start (the game's scheduler is busy at that moment) it leaves its "records only" mark behind, so the save you ask for next runs as a records-only save. The fix is a small change to the game's code at the one place where a save gets turned away: the mark is cleared right there, as the game clears it after any save that runs, so the clear save shows its file list. It is put in once as the game starts and does nothing until a save is turned away; your records are saved with the clear save. Any other save the game turns away the same way no longer spills into the next one either.
[*]While the panel is up the game lets go of the mouse and the panel draws its own pointer. [b]F7[/b] hides or shows it. The cheat switches are remembered from one start to the next.
[/list]

[size=5][b]Install[/b][/size]

Open the game's [b]Bin32[/b] folder (the one with bio4.exe - in Steam: right-click the game, Manage, Browse local files, then Bin32):
[list=1]
[*]Rename the stock [b]steam_api.dll[/b] to [b]steam_api_orig.dll[/b]
[*]Drop this mod's [b]steam_api.dll[/b] in its place
[/list]

To uninstall: delete steam_api.dll and rename steam_api_orig.dll back.

AshleyGodMode   = 0
OneHitKills     = 0
InfiniteAmmo    = 0
SteadyAim       = 0
NoSaveCount     = 0
InfiniteMoney   = 0
NoMissCount     = 0
NoDeathCount    = 0
AutoQTE         = 0
AutoGallery     = 0
EndlessCombo    = 0
AutoKill        = 0
BonusTime       = 0
FreezePlaytime  = 0
FreezeCountdown = 0
[/code]

Lives in [b]RE4OGCabbyCodes.ini[/b] next to bio4.exe (created on first run). [b]RE4OGCabbyCodes.log[/b] beside it records what the mod found and did - attach it (and RE4OGCabbyCodes.prev.log) when reporting a problem. [b]RE4OGCabbyCodes.itembox.ini[/b] holds the main game's item box and [b]RE4OGCabbyCodes.itembox.separate-ways.ini[/b] Separate Ways', one plain-text line per entry.
